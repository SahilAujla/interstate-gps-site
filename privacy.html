Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Interstate GPS is an iPhone app for truck-safe routing. This policy explains what it collects and why. We keep it to what the app needs to work.

What the app collects

  • Your location, while you use the app, to find routes from where you are, give turn-by-turn directions and show nearby truck stops and parking. Locations are sent to our server and our map data providers only to answer those requests. We don't keep a history of where you've been.
  • Your truck's details (height, weight, length and similar), sent with route requests so routes fit your truck. They're stored on your iPhone.
  • Your trips (saved and recent destinations). They're stored on your iPhone only.
  • An anonymous app ID, created when you first use the app, and a daily count of how many routes and searches it makes. We use this to keep the service fair and running.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address from your Google account, so we can identify your account. Signing in is optional.

How it's used

Only to run the app: routing, place search, keeping usage within limits, and your account if you sign in. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

Who processes it

Our server runs on Supabase (hosting and sign-in). Routes and place searches are answered by TomTom and by services using OpenStreetMap data. Maps are shown with Apple Maps. Google handles Google sign-in. Each gets only what it needs for its part.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Account → Delete Account to permanently delete your account. Truck details and trips stay on your iPhone until you delete the app.

Children

Interstate GPS is meant for professional drivers and isn't directed at children under 13.
