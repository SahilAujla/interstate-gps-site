Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Interstate GPS is an iPhone app for truck-safe routing. This policy explains what it collects and why. We keep it to what the app needs to work.

What the app collects

  • Your location, while you use the app (including in the background while you navigate), to find routes from where you are, give turn-by-turn directions and alerts, and show truck stops, parking and other places nearby. Locations are sent to our server and our map data providers only to answer those requests. We don't keep a history of where you've been.
  • Reports you choose to send: weigh station open/closed, road events (for example a crash or police ahead), truck parking and diesel prices. Each report is stored with its location, time and your app ID so other drivers can see it and we can limit abuse. Other drivers see the report, not who sent it.
  • Reviews you post about places, shown to other drivers with your first name and last initial. You can delete your own reviews.
  • Your truck's details (height, weight, length and similar), sent with route requests so routes fit your truck. They're stored on your iPhone.
  • Your trips and saved places. They're stored on your iPhone only.
  • An anonymous app ID, created when you first use the app, and a daily count of how many routes and searches it makes. We use this to keep the service fair and running.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: your name and email address from that account (with Apple you can hide your email), so we can identify your account. Signing in is optional; it's only needed to post reviews.

How it's used

Only to run the app: routing, place search, driver reports and reviews, keeping usage within limits, and your account if you sign in. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

Who processes it

Our server runs on Supabase (hosting, database and sign-in). Routes, traffic and some place searches are answered by TomTom, other places come from Apple Maps and from public data such as OpenStreetMap. Maps are shown with Apple Maps. Apple and Google handle their own sign-in. Each gets only what it needs for its part.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Account → Delete Account to permanently delete your account, together with your reviews and reports. Truck details, trips and saved places stay on your iPhone until you delete the app.

Children

Interstate GPS is meant for professional drivers and isn't directed at children under 13.
