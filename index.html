Interstate GPS

Truck-safe routing for iPhone.

Enter your truck's height, weight, length and hazmat details, search a destination, and get a route that avoids low bridges, weight limits and roads closed to trucks, with live traffic and voice turn-by-turn directions.

It also shows truck stops, diesel, rest areas, truck parking and weigh stations around you and along your route.

Interstate GPS is in early testing with a small group of drivers.
